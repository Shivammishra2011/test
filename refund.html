Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Advance Payments

A non-refundable advance payment is required to begin all custom design projects. This covers the initial research, strategy, and concept development stages.

2. Cancellations Before Concepts

If you cancel the project before any design concepts have been delivered, we will issue a partial refund of the advance payment (minus any administrative or research hours already spent).

3. Cancellations After Concepts

Once the initial design concepts have been presented to you, the advance payment is completely non-refundable. The work has been completed and delivered.

4. Final Files

Final, unwatermarked design files will only be delivered once the full remaining balance has been paid.
